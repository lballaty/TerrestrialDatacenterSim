Model integrity notes
v0.4 (step 5): generation option catalog (21 rows, §8a) with LCOE at the site, resource layer stub per site, supply mix with seven templates and a merit-order dispatch per operating year, firm-equivalent $/MWh feeding the spine, on-site generation capex in the stack, time-to-power from the mix as a candidate binding term, two new self-tests.
v0.3: custom sites (save in browser, export/import as jurisdiction-style JSON), curated data/jurisdictions.json replaces embedded presets on a served origin, lat/lon edits, data-test-* tags on every interactive element and readout, two new self-tests.
v0.2 (step 1–2): shell, input registry with type labels, location presets, derived PUE, composed CAPEX stack, itemised opex, straight-line compute salvage, time-to-power with named binding term, tornado, break-even, self-tests, specification viewer, responsive layout. Grid-only power; single compute row; no catalogs, demand, risk or cohorts yet.
Parity: with the facility basis set to "single $M/MW", opex to "single %", manual PUE 1.3, tariff $80 flat, availability 99.5% manual, horizon 10, spares 10%, 1 MW productive and refresh 3, the spine reproduces the orbital model's terrestrial benchmark of $0.247 / 1M tokens (self-test).
Composition: the §10.2 stack at US-average, Tier III, air cooling composes to ≈ $12M / MW IT, the orbital stub's single figure (self-test).

This is an exploratory scenario model, not a siting study, engineering design, tariff quotation or investment recommendation.
